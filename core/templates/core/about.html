About LMS Nepal

Empowering cooperatives with modern financial management solutions.

Who We Are

LMS Nepal is a Loan Management System designed specifically for cooperative societies in Nepal. Our mission is to simplify loan processing, enhance transparency, and provide digital tools for better financial management. By combining technology with cooperative values, we aim to strengthen community-driven development.

Our Mission

To empower cooperatives across Nepal with digital solutions that make loan management simple, secure, and efficient.

Our Vision

A financially inclusive Nepal where every cooperative has the tools to manage loans effectively and support its members’ growth.

Our Core Values

  • ✅ Transparency
  • ✅ Trust
  • ✅ Community Empowerment
  • ✅ Innovation
